About

I'm Madhuja Gosavi and I paint with acrylics. My work goes from quiet and detailed to bold and a little wild. Animals, seascapes, things that catch my eye and refuse to let go.

I am also starting to explore resin art and clay. New mediums but the same feeling behind every piece.
