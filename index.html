About

I'm Madhuja Gosavi, an artist based in India working primarily in acrylics. My work spans the vivid and the quiet — from deep ocean creatures to golden sunsets at sea — each piece painted with texture, feeling, and a little wildness.

Beyond acrylics, I'm expanding into raising art and clay — new mediums, same soul.
